Privacy Policy

Battery Trace for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Support Privacy Policy 简体中文

In short: Battery Trace does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code. Your battery history stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Who we are

Battery Trace is developed by Fan Tian (“we”). If you have a question about this policy, write to fayne.tien@gmail.com.

What Battery Trace stores, and where

Everything below is stored on your device, in Battery Trace’s own storage:

  • Battery readings: the time, battery level and charging status, recorded while the app is open, by its widgets, and with Battery Trace Pro in the background.
  • Battery health snapshots: maximum capacity, cycle count, full-charge and design capacity, temperature and, on Mac, voltage and current, with the date they apply to.
  • Imported logs: for each Analytics log you share, its date, file name and a fingerprint used to skip it if you share it again.
  • Your devices: model, platform, battery design capacity and a random identifier, used to tell your devices apart when they sync. It is not linked to you or your Apple Account.
  • Your settings, such as import reminders and menu bar options.

Battery Trace does not record your location, what you do on your device, or any other content.

Analytics logs

On iPhone and iPad, battery health comes from the Analytics log that the system writes on your device. Battery Trace reads a log only when you share it with the app or pick it in the Files app. It reads the log on your device and keeps only the battery values listed above, not the rest of the log or a copy of the file. Turning on Share iPhone Analytics or Share iPad Analytics also shares analytics with Apple; that is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, and Battery Trace receives nothing from it.

Mac

On a Mac, Battery Trace reads battery information from macOS, such as level, cycle count, capacity, temperature and power draw. It only reads this information and doesn’t change any system setting. Open at login is used only if you turn it on.

iCloud

With Battery Trace Pro, your battery history and devices are synced through Apple’s iCloud to your private iCloud database, so they appear on your other devices and come back after a reinstall or on a new device. Only you can access this data; we cannot see it. iCloud is provided by Apple and covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

You can stop syncing by turning off iCloud for Battery Trace in the Settings app, and delete the synced data in your iCloud storage settings.

Notifications, widgets and Shortcuts

Import reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Widgets show a summary that Battery Trace saves on your device. Shortcuts automations that record battery events are created by you and run on your device. None of these send anything to us.

Purchases

Battery Trace Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details and no personal information about you. The app asks the App Store only whether Battery Trace Pro is unlocked for your Apple Account or through Family Sharing.

Data we collect

None. Battery Trace has no servers, sends no data to us or to anyone else, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated App Store and crash statistics if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device’s settings.

Your choices

You can export your history as CSV files in Battery Trace’s settings at any time. Deleting Battery Trace removes its data from that device; synced data can be deleted as described under iCloud.

Children

Battery Trace collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
